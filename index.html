Horror Experience Advisory

Shadows of Freddy's is a psychological horror game featuring intense and potentially disturbing content. This experience is designed to create suspense, fear, and anxiety through various audiovisual techniques.

Content Warnings

⚡
Photosensitivity Warning

Contains flashing lights, strobe effects, and rapid visual changes that may trigger seizures in individuals with photosensitive epilepsy.

🔊
Audio Intensity

Features sudden loud sounds, jump scares, ambient horror audio, and high-intensity sound effects.

🌙
Visual Atmosphere

Includes dark environments, limited visibility, disturbing imagery, and atmospheric horror elements.

💭
Psychological Elements

Designed to induce stress, anxiety, fear, and tension through suspenseful gameplay mechanics.

Health & Safety

Not Recommended For:

  • Individuals with photosensitive epilepsy or seizure disorders
  • People with heart conditions or cardiac issues
  • Those experiencing anxiety disorders or PTSD
  • Children under 13 years of age
  • Pregnant individuals or those with high blood pressure

If you experience dizziness, nausea, discomfort, or any adverse symptoms, discontinue play immediately and consult a healthcare professional if necessary.
